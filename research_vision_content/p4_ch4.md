**The Scientific Coordinate System**

**A Universal Language for Organizing Interdisciplinary Research**

*"Assigning every research activity a unique position within an
integrated scientific ecosystem."*

**Why a Scientific Coordinate System?**

Every mature scientific discipline has developed systems for organizing
knowledge.

Biology uses taxonomic classification.

Chemistry uses the periodic table.

Genetics uses chromosome maps.

Geography uses coordinates.

Libraries use cataloguing systems.

Software engineering uses modular architectures.

As interdisciplinary research becomes increasingly important, a similar
organizational system is needed for scientific ideas.

The Universal Research Framework therefore proposes a **Scientific
Coordinate System**.

Its purpose is not merely to classify publications.

Its purpose is to reveal relationships among research questions across
disciplines.

**The Central Principle**

Every research activity can be described by answering six fundamental
questions.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Question**                                  **Dimension**
  --------------------------------------------- -------------------------
  Why is this research being conducted?         Grand Research Program

  How is the problem being viewed?              Universal Framework

  Where is the framework being applied?         Scientific Domain

  What specific question is being investigated? Research Theme

  How is the question being studied?            Methodology

  What knowledge is produced?                   Scientific Output
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

These six dimensions define the location of every research activity
within the Universal Research Framework.

**The Coordinate Format**

A complete coordinate may be written as

P2-F2-AI-T03-COMP-A01

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

The coordinate therefore captures the conceptual location of the work
rather than merely its publication order.

**Beyond Individual Articles**

The same system can organize

-   doctoral theses,

-   research laboratories,

-   interdisciplinary centres,

-   national programmes,

-   collaborative networks.

The framework therefore scales naturally from individual studies to
institutional research planning.

**Advantages of the Coordinate System**

The proposed system offers several potential advantages.

**Conceptual Clarity**

Every research activity has a clearly defined intellectual location.

**Interdisciplinary Navigation**

Researchers can identify related work across different domains.

**Strategic Planning**

Future projects can be planned systematically rather than independently.

**Portfolio Analysis**

Research programmes can evaluate conceptual strengths, gaps, and
opportunities.

**Collaboration**

Potential collaborators can identify complementary expertise using
shared conceptual coordinates rather than disciplinary labels alone.

**Future Directions**

The Scientific Coordinate System is proposed as an evolving
organizational tool.

Future developments may include

-   visual research maps,

-   dynamic knowledge graphs,

-   AI-assisted research planning,

-   semantic navigation of interdisciplinary literature,

-   integration with digital research repositories.

Its usefulness should ultimately be judged by whether it improves the
organization, communication, and conduct of interdisciplinary scientific
research.
