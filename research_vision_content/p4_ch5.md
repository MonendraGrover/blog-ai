**The Universal Research Matrix**

**Integrating Vision, Programs, Frameworks, Domains, and Scientific
Workflows**

*"A multidimensional architecture for organizing interdisciplinary
scientific research."*

**Why Another Matrix?**

Matrices are widely used throughout science.

Biologists organize genes into expression matrices.

Ecologists study species interaction matrices.

Computer scientists use adjacency matrices.

Engineers employ design matrices.

However, these matrices generally describe **relationships within a
single discipline**.

The Universal Research Matrix serves a different purpose.

It is designed to organize **interdisciplinary scientific research**.

Rather than classifying data, it classifies **scientific inquiry**.

**From Classification to Navigation**

Traditional matrices answer

**What belongs where?**

The Universal Research Matrix answers

**Where does this research fit within the broader scientific
ecosystem?**

This distinction is fundamental.

The matrix functions less like a spreadsheet and more like a
**scientific map**.

**The Matrix as a Research Planning Tool**

The Universal Research Matrix can be used to

-   identify unexplored intersections,

-   organize research portfolios,

-   plan future publications,

-   visualize interdisciplinary collaborations,

-   identify conceptual gaps,

-   design educational programmes,

-   structure long-term research strategies.

For example, a researcher may discover that while "Information in
Biology" has been explored extensively, "Architecture in Education"
remains comparatively underdeveloped.

The framework therefore helps identify opportunities as well as organize
existing work.

**Design Principles**

The Universal Research Matrix has been designed to satisfy six
objectives.

**Clarity** -- Makes interdisciplinary research easier to visualize.

**Integration** -- Connects multiple scientific perspectives.

**Scalability** -- Applies to individuals, laboratories, institutes, and
national programmes.

**Extensibility** -- Accommodates new disciplines and frameworks.

**Operational Value** -- Supports planning as well as description.

**Adaptability** -- Encourages continual refinement as science evolves.

**A Proposed Framework**

The Universal Research Matrix should be viewed as a **proposed
conceptual framework**, not as a finalized taxonomy.

Its value will depend on whether it proves useful in practice.

Future applications, empirical evaluations, and feedback from
researchers across disciplines may suggest refinements, extensions, or
alternative organizational structures.

Scientific frameworks mature through use.

This framework is offered in that spirit.
